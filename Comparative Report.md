### **1. Getrella (Rella)** 

### **Primary Focus** 

#### **Content Planning, Collaboration & Influencer Workflow Management** 

### **What It Manages** 

- Visual content calendar 

- Multi-platform post scheduling 

- Team collaboration & task management 

- Client approval workflows 

- Project-based content organization 

- Influencer campaign coordination (basic) 

- Shareable reports 

### **Limitations** 

- No influencer discovery marketplace 

- No influencer payment or contract handling 

- No chat or DM automation 

### **Best For** 

Agencies Content teams Influencer campaign coordination (workflow-focused) 

### **2. Metricool** 

### **Primary Focus** 

#### **Social Media Analytics, Scheduling & Ads Performance** 

### **What It Manages** 

- Post scheduling across major platforms 

- Deep analytics (engagement, reach, growth) 

- Competitor analysis 

- Ad tracking (Meta, Google, TikTok) 

- Link-in-bio tool 

- Professional PDF/PPT reports 

### **Limitations** 

- No influencer management 

- No chatbot or DM automation 

- No CRM features 

### **Best For** 

Data-driven marketers Agencies Social media analysts

### **Primary Focus**

### **What It Manages**

- Media & file management 

- Image watermarking 

- Basic performance analytics 

- Multi-client handling for agencies 

### **Limitations** 

- No advanced analytics 

- No influencer marketing tools 

- No automation or CRM 

### **Best For** 

Small agencies Businesses with basic posting needs

Lead generation Influencers & creators E-commerce businesses

# **Strategic Insight (Key Takeaway)** 

For a UK-based influencer marketing & social media management web app, no single tool fully covers all needs. 

### **Best Strategy** 

#### Build a **combined platform** inspired by: 

- **Getrella** → Content & approval workflows 

- **Metricool** → Analytics & ROI tracking 

- **ManyChat** → DM & lead automation 

- **GoHighLevel** → CRM, automation & white-label SaaS 

### **Result** 

#### A **next-generation Influencer Marketing Platform** that manages: 

- Content & scheduling 

- Influencers & campaigns 

- Analytics & ROI 

- Chat-based lead generation 

- CRM & automation 

- Client & agency dashboards
